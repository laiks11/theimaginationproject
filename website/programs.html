Programs

What you can join today, and where we are headed.

Every program is anchored in one conviction: the ability to think independently and lead with imagination has to be actively built. Here is what runs now, and the roadmap for everything next.

Now · Available today

Programs you can join this month.

Our first sector is live. Everything here runs on tech policy, online and across Texas.

Now

Tech Policy Clubs

Weekly · after school

School chapters meet to learn how technology gets governed, then build a local campaign around the AI Transparency Notice.

Now

Dear Future Leader

Curriculum · school visits

Our flagship assembly curriculum, already delivered to more than 500 elementary students across Texas.

Now

Speaker Seminars & Forums

Monthly · public

Hosted panels of experts in conversation with teens, recorded and archived as free content for anyone.

The roadmap

One sector a year, taught as one connected way of thinking.

We add a new research-backed field each year. Every path connects students to the leaders of that industry.

Tech & Cyber PolicyLive

Advocacy, clubs, and seminars on the rules shaping technology.

Medicine & Health2027

Health-policy literacy and a real look at a life in medicine.

Business2028

How markets, money, and ventures actually get built.

Service2028

Civic leadership rooted in the place you actually live.

Arts2029

Creative practice as a discipline of its own.

The 2030 vision

A full K-12 pipeline and a national network of interdisciplinary young thinkers.

On the horizon

What we are building toward.

As we grow, the program becomes a full experience that follows a student from their first club meeting to a seat at the table.

The big build

An after-school platform with interactive modules for every grade.

Our first website-led curriculum will teach each sector through interactive modules built for each grade level, so a nine-year-old and a seventeen-year-old both get work pitched exactly to them. Chapters run it after school, and any student can follow along online.

Summer camps

Immersive weeks in cities where each sector actually lives.

College + career prep

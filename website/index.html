The bill we're backing · AI Transparency Notice

When a school uses AI to make or substantially inform a decision about a student, that student should be told AI was involved, and should have access to a human who can review it.

When an algorithm flags an essay or denies an accommodation, a young person deserves to know a machine was involved, and to reach a real person who can take a second look.

03

Four commitments we build with real mechanisms, not slogans.

01

Intellectual courage

We put students in front of real speakers and give them the mic, so they learn to sit with hard questions in public.

02

Human agency

Every speaking opportunity is theirs to own. Students lead the room, ask the questions, and carry the argument.

03

Equity

We provide free materials to clubs, targeting rural areas and developing cities first, and eventually developing countries.

04

Cultural curiosity

Global thinking is built into every mentor-led discussion, so no student learns from a single point of view.

04

One sector a year, taught as one connected way of thinking.

Tech policy is the first thread. Each year we add another field and connect students to the people who lead it, building interdisciplinary thinkers from a young age.

Questions

Good questions, answered.

Our core programs serve students ages 4 to 18. High schoolers can also join as volunteer mentors, and adults eighteen and older are welcome as mentors, speakers, and supporters.

Many of our resources are free and available in four languages. For programs with a fee, scholarship seats are reserved for students from under-resourced communities, and livestream access to our events is always free.

Yes. We welcome mentors, speakers, and volunteers, and new mentors are recruited every month. Reach us at contact@theimaginationproject.org to start a conversation.

Educators can deliver our materials in their classrooms, host events, and open The Imagination Project as a club. We provide classroom-ready materials with minimal prep and a responsive point of contact.

Donations of any size help us provide one-on-one mentorship and keep our resources free. You can also subscribe to our Substack, follow our work, and share it.

Get involved

Help TIP redefine education.

Any and all help is impactful, we are eager to strengthen the movement.

01 · Translate 02 · Start a chapter 03 · Support research 04 · Volunteer · Media 05 · Partner
01

Translate information on technology

Join our student translator corps carrying AI education into as many languages as possible. You pick the language and the pace; we provide the source material, review, and credit.

✳ Earn verified service hours
02

Start a chapter

Bring TIP to your school, city, or state (global applications welcome): run translation drives, group projects, and AI literacy sessions with our playbook, materials, and a direct line to our team.

✳ Playbook + mentorship included
03

Support the research

We're building an index that scores how colleges use and rely on AI: with data scientists, surveys, and investigative rigor. Researchers, data folks, and survey respondents are all encouraged to help.

✳ Expanding across US colleges
04

Volunteer — media & more

Filmmakers, designers, editors, social storytellers, event hands. Share your specialty and how many hours you've got to spare for our cause.

✳ Remote-friendly
05

Partner with TIP

Companies, school districts, nonprofits, and funders: co-sponsor translation drives, pilot our programs, or show your support for the AI index.

✳ Districts & companies welcome

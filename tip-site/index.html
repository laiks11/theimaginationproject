AI is rewriting the world in one language. We are a youth-led nonprofit driving every person to protect our education, and support those without the same resources.

What we do

The Imagination Project translates digital literacy education into the languages of underresourced communities — from elementary classrooms to developing countries — and builds the research, mentorship, and programs that give young people agency over digital safety.

The work

How does TIP enable change?

01

Translate

Students translate AI education into as many languages as possible — earning service hours while closing the digital divide for kids and communities neglected by the AI boom.

 Active now 02

The AI Index

Investigative research with data scientists, building a precise index that scores how colleges use and rely on AI — designed to address the issue of AI accountability across US campuses.

 In development 03

The Education Hub

Speakers, podcasts, and career-path learning to support irreplaceable human skills: featuring mentorship, multi-cultural discussion, long-term projects, and advocacy.

Dear Future Leader

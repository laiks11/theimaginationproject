One book, two continents
A children's book written for Texas classrooms now funds literacy and hygiene programs for eight hundred women half a world away.

Every copy of the Dear Future Leader book routes its proceeds to Manav Sadhna in Ahmedabad, India — proof that a program built by young people can carry further than anyone expected.

Where this is going

2025

Founded — and moving fast

TIP is founded in Texas. Dear Future Leader is written, and delivered to 500+ elementary students within four months. The book begins funding 800+ women through Manav Sadhna.

2026

The translator corps

Students begin translating AI education toward our 50+ language goal — earning service hours while closing the digital divide. Anabella Laudicina joins as Executive Director.
